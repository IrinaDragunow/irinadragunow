## Recent work

**Greenfield ERP program, German HVAC enterprise (Nov 2025 – Oct 2026)**
Transformation program manager and AI automation lead for a self-hosted ERP platform with a GraphQL API layer; 15+ people across Germany, Pakistan and Vietnam.

- **Operating model from zero:** Jira and Confluence structure with one owner per domain, one ticket template, routing and priority rules, QA process, Scrum rhythm, weekly executive reporting.
- **Bug triage pipeline** (n8n, 16 nodes): Sentry → Claude → Jira → Slack. Classified, translated ticket with steps to reproduce in under 10 seconds instead of 3–6 minutes of manual work.
- **Slack-to-Jira intake** (n8n, 48 nodes): AI proposes type, position in the hierarchy, priority and owner; rules enforce the structure; duplicate check. Used daily by 14 people.
- **Delivery Health Monitor** (Python): cycle time, bottlenecks, bus factor and reopen rate, bilingual dashboard three times a day with snapshots as an audit trail.
- **Measured result**, four months before vs. three months after the process reset: tickets done within two weeks 27% → 81%, median time to done 29 → 6 days, backlog older than 30 days 134 → 17, reopen rate flat at 4–5%. Automation was one of several levers; the team grew in the same period.

Everything was documented, handed over and is still in use. The code belongs to the client, so it is not published here.

**GDPR case management, German LegalTech company (Nov 2025 – Apr 2026)**
Mapped and redesigned the case workflow for 500+ active claims; case system on Airtable with routing automated in Make; classification rules and SOPs. Operated by the internal team.
